Ascended Heroes on a Budget Returns Tomorrow! 💸

August 27, 2026By PokéDaé

Mega Meganium ex, Mega Emboar ex and Mega Feraligatr ex are returning in three new Ascended Heroes tins, TOMORROW - August 28!

Each $21.99 tin includes:

🔥 4 Ascended Heroes booster packs

🔥 1 foil Mega Evolution promo

🔥 4 opportunities to get hurt by the greatest Pokémon set ever 😎

That works out to roughly $5.50 per pack - and with loose Ascended Heroes packs still selling way above that, these tins might be one of the best chances we’ve had to open this set on a budget.

Just know that the promos are reprints from the Mega ex Boxes released in January, and you’re basically paying for four packs and getting a cool metal lunchbox for your basic cards and tears. 😂

Best Buy already has the listing posted on their website with a limit of one and styles varying, but I have not found a confirmed online drop time. Expect these to begin appearing online and in stores EVERYWHERE STARTING TOMORROW. I'm certain these will be going for $50 a pop on the aftermarket - so they will go fast.
