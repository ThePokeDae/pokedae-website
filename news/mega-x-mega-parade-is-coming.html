🚨 Mega x Mega Parade Is Coming!

August 27, 2026By PokéDaé

We just got Mega Zeraora and Mega Darkrai in Pitch Black, Aura Seeker will feature Mega Lucario Z, and now Mega Raichu X and Y are expected to receive their first-ever TCG cards in MEGA x MEGA Parade on February 19.

This is exciting because Raichu hasn’t had a major spotlight card since the Tag Team era, and most of its expensive cards are vintage. I find it strange that Pokémon has always left Raichu in Pikachu's shadow - so I'm excited to see him finally get some love! ⚡

Everything Pokémon does is connected - the games, cards and release schedule are all pieces of the same puzzle. If you’ve played the Legends: Z-A expansion, you probably already have a good idea of what's coming to the TCG!

I’m still crossing my fingers for Mega Absol Z and Mega Garchomp Z to join the set, but I have a feeling Pokémon may save both for a Black Bolt/White Flare-style “Z-Type” set in the near future.

Who else do you want to see in MEGA x MEGA Parade, Trainers?
