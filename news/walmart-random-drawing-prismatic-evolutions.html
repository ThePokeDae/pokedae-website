🚨 Walmart’s First Pokémon Draw Is Tomorrow!

August 30, 2026By PokéDaé

Walmart’s new random drawing system officially kicks off August 31 with Prismatic Evolutions Elite Trainer Boxes for $69.

Drawing opens: 6 PM PDT / 9 PM EDT.

You’ll have a window to enter, and winners are chosen randomly. This is NOT first come, first served — so there’s no advantage to entering the second it opens.

No camping the app and no racing to checkout. Enter during the window and everybody gets a shot.

If this works the way it’s supposed to, this could be a huge upgrade over watching the same bots and resellers vacuum up every online drop in seconds.

Tomorrow we find out if Walmart cooked… or burned the kitchen down. 👀
