🚨 Target Has a Ton of Pokémon Toys, Trainers!

August 28, 2026By PokéDaé

And a lot of them are Target exclusives!

If you’re out shopping for a Pokémon fan and the card aisle looks like it got hit by a tornado, go check the toy aisle. 🌪️ There’s honestly stuff for every age.

I’m pushing 40 and I’d be thrilled with half the stuff in these pictures. 😂 Those Mewtwo, Rayquaza, Blastoise and Garchomp Select figures are AWESOME—and don’t even get me started on the Pokémon LEGO. I could disappear for three days building those things.

That’s something I think gets lost when we spend all day talking about TCG prices, restocks and the market.

Pokémon is EVERYWHERE.

The TCG might have its ups and downs, but the franchise itself is bigger than cardboard. Kids love it. Adults love it. Families love it. And nearly 30 years later, Pokémon is still a massive part of pop culture.

It isn’t going anywhere.
