🚨 Pokémon Just Turned Sleeping Into a Flex!

August 27, 2026By PokéDaé

I've been saying that Pokémon was the new Louis Vuitton, and I truly believe that with this new generation. It's one of the main reasons I don't see this hobby going anywhere but up - Pokémon is simply part of the modern culture. 👜

Google revealed today the Fitbit Air Special Edition Pokémon Sleep, featuring a Sleepy Blue band with a napping Pikachu and direct integration with Pokémon Sleep.

It tracks your sleep, connects it to the game and rewards you for doing the one thing most of us Trainers aren't doing much of anyway, so it's kind of pointless. 😂

Preorders are open now ahead of its September release. I'm not sure how many Trainers love their sleep as much as my wife - but I bet she'll be battling y'all atop those leaderboards! 😴
