❤️ This Is What the Pokémon Community Is All About.

August 27, 2026By PokéDaé

A follower in Georgia reached out because her fiancé’s birthday was coming up. Product is tough to find in their area, so she asked if I could help put together some sealed packs for him to rip.

Mission accepted. 🫡

I sent an Ascended Heroes Feraligatr ex Box, a Pokémon Day 2026 Pikachu Collection, a Destined Rivals/Journey Together Two-Pack Blister and a few singles.

Yesterday, she messaged me after they opened everything.

That ONE Journey Together pack traveled from Franklin, Indiana, all the way to Georgia with Lillie’s Clefairy ex inside, the chase card from the entire set. Absolutely wild!

I had a blast putting this birthday surprise together, but hearing about that pull made it even better. Mission officially accomplished.

Sometimes it really isn’t about the value of the cardboard. It’s about helping someone create a memory they’ll never forget. She said this was his first ever time pulling a chase card. I'd say that's something he will remember forever. ❤️
