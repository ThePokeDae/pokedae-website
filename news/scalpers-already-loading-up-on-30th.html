🚨 Scalpers Are Already Loading Up on 30th!

August 28, 2026By PokéDaé

Reports are circulating that Pokémon may be printing around 180 MILLION 30th Celebration packs. That sounds like a lot—until you see an entire car stuffed with unreleased Ultra-Premium Collections.

These UPCs don’t officially release until NOVEMBER, yet this circulating photo appears to show someone with a vehicle full of them. They’re currently valued at more than $500 each on the secondary market.

So tell me again how a massive print run automatically means everyone will find this set at retail?

Pokémon can print millions of boxes, but it won’t matter if people are getting them months early and hoarding them by the carload before Trainers ever get a chance.

Where did all of these come from? How were they obtained this early? And why does this keep happening with 30th Celebration?

I’ve been saying it—this set could be printed into the ground and still disappear the second it touches a shelf. Supply means nothing when greed gets there first.

This is ridiculous, Trainers. November isn’t even close.
