Privacy

Your information stays yours.

When you join the PokéDaé newsletter and PDI beta list, we collect the email address you provide and, optionally, your first name.

How we use it

We use this information only to send PDI beta updates, Pokémon TCG news, event information, newsletters, and restock calendars you requested.

How it is handled

Brevo processes and stores subscriber information on our behalf. PokéDaé does not sell your information. We share it only with services needed to operate the email list.

Your choices

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You may also request access, correction, or deletion of your information by emailing dae@pokedae.com.
