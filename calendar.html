WORLDWIDESEP 16SIMULTANEOUS LAUNCH

Pokémon’s major 30th-anniversary expansion launches worldwide on the same day. Here’s the important stuff every Trainer and parent needs in one quick scan.

01Inside Every Pack

Five foil cards—including one of 30 unique Pikachu illustrations—plus one foil Basic Energy.

02When You Can Play

Legal in Pokémon TCG Live on September 15 and for tournament play beginning September 30.

03One Global Release

North America, Japan and other territories receive the expansion together on September 16.
