THE POKÉDAÉ RESTOCK RULES

Get cards.
Keep your sanity.

01

Learn your store. A repeated local pattern is more useful than somebody else's schedule three states away.

02

Don't assume one miss means sold out. Stock can arrive later, get held, or hit another day.

03

Never turn cardboard into a safety issue. No pushing, racing, blocking aisles or acting ridiculous over a box.

04

Buy the product, not the emotion. If the hunt starts feeling desperate, skip it. There will always be another drop.
