FAMILY TRAINER GUIDE

GO together.
Keep it fun.

Pokémon GO is at its best when everybody gets outside, laughs a little, catches something cool and makes it home with a story.

01BEFORE YOU LEAVE

Charge the phone, pack water, check the weather and decide where everyone is meeting if the group gets separated.

02EVENT TICKETS

Read the bonuses before buying. Free event gameplay is often plenty for younger or casual Trainers.

03RAID DAYS

Pick a safe public route with several Gyms instead of driving from raid to raid.

04THE POKÉDAÉ RULE

The catch is never worth crossing a road unsafely. The Pokémon will come back. You only get one Trainer.
