IN-HAND CARD PREVIEW
SET INTELLIGENCE

What makes each
pack different?

ONE 30TH PACK5 foil cards—including one Pikachu+1 foil Basic Energy
  • Every card in the expansion receives a foil treatment.
  • The Classic Collection revisits 30 recognizable cards from TCG history.
  • A new rarity joins illustration rares, special illustration rares and Pokémon ex.
  • Booster packs are available inside collection products—not as standard individual sleeved boosters.

PLAY NOTE Cards enter Pokémon TCG Live on September 15 and become tournament legal beginning September 30. Classic Collection reprints are not automatically legal unless an already-legal version exists.
