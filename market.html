REBOUNDING
MARKET CONTEXT

A dip isn't
a disaster.

The hobby loves extremes: everything is either “going to the moon” or “crashing.” The 30th is proving in real time why that usually makes no sense. One product can rebound while the box right next to it keeps cooling. Watch the item — not the panic post.

“Buy the product, not the emotion.”
01

Release hype inflates prices.

Early listings can reflect scarcity and excitement more than sustainable demand.

02

Supply changes everything.

A product can fall sharply and still be extremely healthy once more inventory reaches buyers.

03

One week is not a verdict.

Seven-day movement is useful context, not a release-day forecast or a crystal ball.

04

Collectors create the floor.

Long-term strength comes from cards and products people actually want to own.

NO
FOMO
THE POKÉDAÉ MARKET RULES

Numbers matter.
So does context.

01

Don't chase green arrows. A price going up is not automatically a reason to buy.

02

Corrections are healthy. Cooling can create better entry points and shake out manufactured hype.

03

Cards over cardboard. Sealed value matters, but the art and cards inside are what create lasting collector demand.

04

Have a reason. Collect it, rip it, hold it, gift it — just know why you are buying before the market tells you how to feel.
