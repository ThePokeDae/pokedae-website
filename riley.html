WHY WE'RE DOING THIS

Sometimes a little joy
goes a long way.

For a kid spending time in a hospital, opening a Pokémon pack or hugging a new plush can create a few minutes where the hospital fades into the background and they're just a Trainer having fun.

This one means something to us.

This drive is our chance to take everything we love about Pokémon — collecting, connecting and sharing the excitement — and turn it into something that can make a real difference for Riley kids and families. PokéDaé has always been about people first, and this is that idea in action.

THE WAGON IS ALREADY ROLLING

Our community showed up.
Jason Paige made it even bigger.

Rips for Riley Kids started with our own community stepping in — and then Jason Paige joined the story in a huge way.

❤️ COMMUNITY FIRST

Pokémon Family Community + Jason Paige

We already have donations coming in from the Pokémon Family Community, and we are incredibly excited to share that Jason Paige — the voice behind the original Pokémon theme song — donated 200 custom cards for the kids as part of Rips for Riley Kids. He also included a separate group of signed, personalized Pokémon cards in the package.

THE REAL STORY

From the beginning, we had hoped Jason could join us in person for the drive, and he was fully on board. With the upcoming birth of his child, the timing and logistics simply did not work out this time. We want that stated clearly because there has been incorrect information circulating about what happened.

Jason was supportive of the event, and even though he could not be here in person, he still found a way to show up in a huge way through his donation. We plan to use the signed and personalized cards to help raise even more for Riley, including the possibility of a silent auction with 100% of the money raised going directly to the Riley fundraiser.
