WHY WE'RE DOING THIS

Sometimes a little joy
goes a long way.

For a kid spending time in a hospital, opening a Pokémon pack or hugging a new plush can create a few minutes where the hospital fades into the background and they're just a Trainer having fun.

This one means something to us.

This drive is our chance to take everything we love about Pokémon — collecting, connecting and sharing the excitement — and turn it into something that can make a real difference for Riley kids and families. PokéDaé has always been about people first, and this is that idea in action.

THE WAGON IS ALREADY ROLLING

The Pokémon Family Community showed up.
Then Jason Paige stepped up BIG.

We already have donations coming in from the Pokémon Family Community, and we are incredibly excited to share that Jason Paige — the voice behind the original Pokémon theme song — donated more than 200 Pokémon cards to Rips for Riley Kids.

Many of the cards are signed and personalized, making this donation something truly special. We are still deciding exactly how these cards will be used as part of the Riley drive, but having a piece of Pokémon history join a community-driven campaign like this means the world to us — and it perfectly represents what this hobby can be when people choose to give back.
